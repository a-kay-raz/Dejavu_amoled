Ukubukeka okusha: imibala empunga, izithombe zangemuva zezindawo zokusebenza nezithonjana ezintsha. Amakhasi okwamukela asetha i-Dejavu, futhi amathiphu akhombisa izinyakazo ezifihliwe. Ibha yekheli ingaba phezulu noma phansi. Swayipha phezulu ibha yezenzo uthole imenyu entantayo, manje ene-“Ithebhu entsha”, “Londoloza ikhasi” ne-“Sula idatha”. Amathebhu asondela futhi ahlehla esikrinini sasekhaya, ahlala ephapheme uma ushintsha izinhlelo. Kanye nokulayisha okusheshayo ne-Firefox 159.
